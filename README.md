# TeachHub Lagos – A Centralised Digital Resource Platform for Educators

## Project Overview

TeachHub Lagos is an educational technology platform designed to help educators access, create, organise, and share curriculum-aligned digital learning resources.

The project explores how a centralised digital platform can address challenges teachers face when searching for, managing, and retrieving educational materials. Lagos State was used as the case study.

**Project type:** Educational Technology (EdTech)  
**My role:** Business Analyst  
**Team size:** 3 members  
**Project status:** Functional proof of concept / academic prototype

**Live application:** https://teachhub-ng-2026.vercel.app

---

## The Business Problem

Teachers often rely on scattered digital platforms, informal networks, and manual processes to find and manage teaching resources.

The project research identified several challenges among the teachers surveyed:

- 80% reported difficulty finding curriculum-aligned digital resources.
- 75% spent four or more hours weekly searching for teaching materials.
- 85% experienced difficulty organising and retrieving lesson materials.
- 65% relied on WhatsApp groups and informal networks to access resources.

These findings highlighted the need for a centralised platform where educators could manage and access learning resources more efficiently.

## Project Objectives

The project aimed to:

1. Provide a centralised platform for educational resources.
2. Enable teachers to create and manage courses and lessons.
3. Improve the organisation and retrieval of teaching materials.
4. Support the attachment of digital learning resources, including images and videos.
5. Provide quiz creation and resource categorisation.
6. Support role-based access for teachers and administrators.
